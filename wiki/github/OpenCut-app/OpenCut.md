# OpenCut-app/OpenCut

The open-source CapCut alternative — a TypeScript-based video editor in the browser / desktop.

## What it is

A TypeScript video-editing app positioned as a free, OSS alternative to CapCut (TikTok's mainstream consumer video editor). Web-first with desktop builds. Aimed at content creators who want a CapCut-like UX without CapCut's account / cloud sync requirements. MIT-licensed.

## Key features

- Browser-based video editing with timeline, transitions, effects.
- Desktop builds for users who want offline / local-first.
- MIT-licensed.

## Tech stack

- TypeScript primary.

## When to reach for it

- You want a CapCut alternative without sign-in / cloud sync.
- You're shipping content and want a free editor.

## When *not* to reach for it

- You need professional-grade video editing — DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Final Cut are still closer-fit for serious work.
- You need broad codec / format support — verify feature parity before committing.

## Maturity signal

52k stars, 5.6k forks, MIT, actively maintained.

## Alternatives

- CapCut — commercial original.
- DaVinci Resolve — free, professional-grade.
- Kdenlive, Shotcut — established OSS video editors.
