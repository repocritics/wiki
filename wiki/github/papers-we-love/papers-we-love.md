# papers-we-love/papers-we-love

A curated index of CS research papers organized by topic — the canonical "what should I read in distributed systems / type theory / databases" reference.

## What it is

A long-running community-maintained catalog of computer-science academic papers worth reading: distributed systems, databases, programming languages, machine learning, security, networking, theory of computation, and more. Each paper has a brief annotation. Companion local meetup chapter network (paperswelove.org) hosts in-person paper-reading discussions.

## Key features

- Coverage across major CS subfields with curated annotations.
- Local meetup chapter network for in-person discussion.
- Tightly curated — open issues count of 2 is exceptional.

## Tech stack

- Markdown only.
- Shell at the language tag is for repo CI scripts.

## When to reach for it

- You're an engineer looking to read foundational CS papers and want a curated reading list.
- You're starting a paper-reading club at your company.
- You're building a CS curriculum and need a vetted paper anthology.

## When *not* to reach for it

- You want recently-published bleeding-edge papers — the canon skews to foundational.
- You want a license-clean training corpus — SPDX `null`; verify LICENSE.

## Maturity signal

107k stars, 6k forks, last push recent. 11+ years. Open issues = 2.
