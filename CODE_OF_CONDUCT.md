# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We welcome contributions from everyone. We pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for all.

## Standards

✅ **Encouraged**:
- Constructive, citation-backed criticism
- Respectful disagreement
- Focus on the work, not the contributor

❌ **Not tolerated**:
- Personal attacks against repo maintainers or contributors
- Harassment, discrimination
- Spam, off-topic promotion
- Vandalism (mass deletion, defamation)

## Enforcement

Violations escalate to the project owner (Tom) via:
- `[abuse]` tag in PR/issue
- Email: conduct@repocritics.com

Consequences range from PR rejection to temporary or permanent ban from the
RepoCritics platform.

## Maintainer Response Principle

Per the Michelin-guide principle: **subjects don't review themselves**. Repo
maintainers may add a "Maintainer Response" but cannot modify reviews about
their own work. Disputes go to Tom escalation.
